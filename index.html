FENN

Data engineer and film producer. In my spare time I build calm, distraction-free software for the Light Phone III — small tools that feel native to the device and stay out of the way.

Everything below is free and open source.

Light Phone tools

  • Chats — WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram and more, bridged to your Light Phone III.
  • Audiobooks — A minimalist audiobook player for the Light Phone III.
  • Callsin development — 1:1 WhatsApp voice calling on the Light Phone III.
  • iMessagein development — iMessage on the Light Phone III.
  • Passes — Store boarding passes, tickets and loyalty codes with QR/barcode display.
  • Tasks — A calm, local-first to-do list.
  • Recall — Anki-compatible spaced-repetition review tool.
  • WiFi — Captive-portal sign-in and connect-by-QR.
  • Location — One-tap gate for the Light Phone III's location services.
  • Passkey — A passkey authenticator for the Light Phone III.
  • Radio — Online radio player.
  • Stopwatch — A simple stopwatch.

Other

  • open-madrid — An open, permissively-licensed implementation of Apple's iMessage protocol, in Kotlin.
